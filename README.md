# Hi, I'm Sanjana 👋

I'm a data analyst and engineer who sits at the intersection of technical execution and business decision-making. I've worked across CRM analytics, data pipeline engineering and customer journey analysis, building things that actually get used: dashboards that drive same-day decisions, SQL pipelines that feed executive reporting, and segmentation logic that runs across millions of customers.

What makes my work different is that I care as much about the "so what" as the "how". I don't just build the model or query — I make sure the output is something a stakeholder can act on.

---


## What I'm working on

- Regression and classification ML projects in R and Python, focused on building models that answer real business questions rather than just scoring well on metrics
- Optimisation problems including greedy algorithms and simple heuristics, applied to resource allocation and operational planning scenarios
- Tree-based models (CART, bagging, random forests, boosting) with a focus on interpretability so results are explainable to non-technical stakeholders
- Monte Carlo simulation in Python for uncertainty quantification in business forecasting
- Customer segmentation analysis using clustering techniques to identify behavioural patterns and translate them into actionable targeting strategies
- A/B test design and analysis, including sample size planning, significance testing and translating lift results into business recommendations
- Building end-to-end analytics workflows: raw data in, clean insight out, with reproducible code and documented assumptions throughout
- Dashboard design in Power BI focused on what a decision-maker actually needs to see, not just what the data contains
